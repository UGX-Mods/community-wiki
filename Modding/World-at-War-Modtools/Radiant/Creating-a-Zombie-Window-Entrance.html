Window entrances (barricades) are the boarded-up windows zombies tear down to get into the playable area. Players can rebuild the boards for points. Zombies that spawn outside always walk to the nearest window first and only look for players after they have climbed through it (see Window teardown (exterior_goal) ).

Preparation

Plan where the window goes: it connects an outside area, where the zombies spawn, with a room of the playable area. Make sure the room belongs to a zone, so the spawners outside are active when players are in it (see Ultimate Guide to Zones in v1.4 Maps ).

Cut the opening

Cut a gap into the wall where the window should be, like the gap for a door. The bottom of the gap is the window sill the zombies climb over.

Place the barricade prefab

The stock barricade comes as a prefab with everything a window needs (boards, the goal for the zombies and the traversal for the climbing animation):

  1. Right-click in the 2D view and choose misc > prefab . The prefab dialog opens in the _prefabs folder.
  2. Select factory_barricade.map .
  3. Move and rotate the prefab into the opening so the boards face the outside.
  4. Align the top of the traverse brush of the prefab with the bottom of the opening. Otherwise the hands of the zombies do not touch the sill during the climbing animation.

Line the opening with a few brushes as a wooden window frame, so the gap in the wall looks finished.

Pathing and spawners

  • Add path nodes outside the building, so zombies can walk from their spawners to each window and away again.
  • Place the zombie spawners outside, so the zombies have to come through the windows. The spawners of a zone use the targetname <zone_name>_spawners (see Creating Zombie and Dog Spawners ).

Compile the map and check in game that zombies reach the window, tear down the boards and climb through, and that you can rebuild the boards.
