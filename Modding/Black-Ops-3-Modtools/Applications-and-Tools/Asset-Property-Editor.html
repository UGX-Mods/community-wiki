The Asset Property Editor (APE) is the asset editor of the Call of Duty: Black Ops 3 modtools. Every asset the game uses, like models, materials, images, animations, weapons or sounds, is set up in APE before it can be used in Radiant Black or in scripts.

GDT files and assets

APE stores assets in GDT files (game data tables). A GDT can hold many assets of different types. Keep the assets of your map or mod in your own GDT instead of editing the stock ones.

To add an asset:

  1. Open APE from the modtools launcher.
  2. Create a new asset in your GDT.
  3. Enter the asset name and choose the asset type, for example xmodel , xanim , material or image .
  4. Fill in the properties of the asset, like the source file (for example an exported .XMODEL_BIN or .XANIM_BIN file) and its settings. Most properties can keep their default values.
  5. Save the GDT and link your map or mod from the launcher, so the asset is built into it.
