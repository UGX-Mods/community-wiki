Radiant Black is the level editor of the Call of Duty: Black Ops 3 modtools. You build the geometry of your map in it, place models, prefabs, lights and entities, and set up the gameplay with key-value pairs (KVPs), for example zones, spawners and buyable doors.

Starting Radiant Black

Radiant Black is part of the Black Ops 3 modtools, which you install through Steam. Open the modtools launcher, select your map and open it in Radiant. After saving your changes, compile and link the map from the launcher to test it in game.

Compared to the World at War Radiant

If you know Radiant from World at War, most concepts stay the same: brushes, textures, prefabs and entities with KVPs. Many entities, prefabs and KVPs of the zombies mode have changed, so follow the Black Ops 3 guides below instead of World at War tutorials.
