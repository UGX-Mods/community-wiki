Join Discord

On the UGX-Mods Discord you can get in touch with thousands of other custom zombies players and modders, get help with issues and find players to team up with.

You need a Discord account and a UGX-Mods account.

  1. Join the UGX-Mods Discord with the invite link on ugx-mods.com/discord .
  2. Agree to the Discord rules.
  3. Log in on UGX-Mods and link your Discord account to your UGX-Mods account. You need the link to get full access.

Your forum groups are synced to Discord roles once the accounts are linked.

More information: https://www.ugx-mods.com/discord/


(If you have issues with account linking, contact one of our Discord administrators)
